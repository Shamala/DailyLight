# Privacy Policy — Daily Light

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

Daily Light is an Android home-screen widget published by the developer of this app.
This policy explains what the app does with information. It is short because the app
does very little.

## What Daily Light collects

**Nothing.**

Daily Light does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It
has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting
and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, which means Android will not
permit it to make a network connection. No information can leave your device,
because the app has no way to send it.

## What is stored on your phone

Daily Light saves your settings and your own writing in the app's private storage on
your device:

- the year-line style, text size, theme, and evening hour you chose
- whether the colours follow the time of day, and whether the year bar is shown
- any affirmations you wrote yourself
- any lines you marked to be shown more often

This stays on your phone, in storage that other apps cannot read. It is never
uploaded.

If you have Android Backup turned on in your system settings, Android may include
this settings file in the backup it makes to your own Google account, so that your
settings follow you to a new phone. That backup is made and controlled by Android
and by your Google account settings, not by Daily Light. You can turn it off in
**Settings → Google → Backup** on your phone.

## Permissions

Daily Light requests one permission:

- **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED** — so the widget can redraw itself with the correct date
  after you restart your phone. It is used for nothing else.

## Children

Daily Light collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your information

Uninstalling Daily Light removes everything it stored. Affirmations you added and
lines you kept can also be removed individually inside the app at any time.
