## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Daily Light is a widget. There is no app to open every morning, no streak to keep,
no notification asking for your attention. It sits on the home screen and shows you
one thing when you unlock the phone:

• the weekday and the full date
• how far along the year is — a day count and a quiet bar, not a countdown
• an affirmation for the day
• a short thought to sit with

The words hold still. Whatever the card says at seven in the morning, it still says
at four in the afternoon, so it becomes something you recognise rather than
something new to read. At an hour you choose, it turns to a closing voice for the
evening — setting down, noticing what went well, permission to stop.

The colours follow the light: indigo at dawn, cooler through the middle of the day,
rose at dusk, deepest at night. You can turn that off and keep one palette, switch
the whole card to a light theme, and choose the text size.

MAKE IT YOURS

Write your own affirmations and they join the rotation alongside the ones that come
with the app. When a line lands, mark it and it comes back around every few days
instead of rarely. Tap the words on the widget to draw a different pairing — the
reshuffle lasts the day, and tomorrow returns to its own words.

NO ACCOUNT, NO INTERNET, NO TRACKING

Daily Light has no sign-in, makes no network requests, and collects nothing. It has
no analytics and no advertising. Everything you write stays in the app's own storage
on your phone. The only permission it asks for is the one that lets the date be
correct again after you restart the phone.

It works on the home screen, and on the lock screen where your phone allows widgets
there. Resize it to the shape that suits your home screen.

All the writing in the app is original. There are no quotations to attribute and
nothing borrowed.
```
